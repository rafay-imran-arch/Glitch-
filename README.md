## Glitch Up - Version 1 : A short Game

About a person Glitch Character who has to go up and try to escape! 
Version 1 -- very dumb and bad level design 
but great movement and control 

### CONTROLS are
A - Move Left <br>
D - Move Right <br>
Space - Jump  <br>
Space Space - Double Jump <br>
Hold SPACE in air = Float<br>
